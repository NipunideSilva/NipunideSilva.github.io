I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at Clarkson University, specializing in **data-driven dynamical systems** and **scientific machine learning**. My research focuses on understanding and modeling collective behavior in biological systems through the lens of deterministic models and modern computational methods.

I develop mathematical frameworks that combine classical dynamical systems theory with cutting-edge machine learning techniques—including **neural ODEs**, **sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy)**, and **non-parametric statistical methods**—to extract governing equations and predict complex system behavior from data.

**Research Interests:**

- Mathematical modeling of biological systems
- Data-driven discovery of dynamical systems
- Scientific machine learning (neural ODEs, physics-informed ML)
- Sparse system identification and model reduction
- Predictive analytics for complex systems
