
[Dr. James Greene](https://www.jamesgreenelab.org/) is an Assistant Professor in 
the Department of Mathematics at Clarkson University and my PhD advisor. His research 
lies at the intersection of mathematical biology, dynamical systems, and optimal 
control theory, with a particular focus on modeling cancer treatment strategies and 
understanding collective behavior in biological systems.

My dissertation, *Dynamics on Collective Motion*, is developed under his guidance, 
exploring data-driven approaches to learning interaction kernels and environmental 
forces in agent-based systems.
