[Dr. Marko Budišić](https://mbudisic.github.io/) is an Associate Professor in the
Department of Mathematics at Clarkson University. His research focuses on dynamical
systems, ergodic theory, and data-driven methods for understanding complex flows and
transport phenomena.

As my previous advisor, Dr. Budišić guided my early research in persistent homology
applied to two-dimensional vortex dynamics, which was presented as a poster at the
SIAM Conference on Applications of Dynamical Systems (DS23) in Portland, 2023.
