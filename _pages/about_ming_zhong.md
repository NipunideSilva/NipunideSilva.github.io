[Dr. Ming Zhong](https://mingjzhong.github.io/) is an Assistant Professor in the 
Department of Mathematics at Johns Hopkins University and a research collaborator 
on my dissertation, *Dynamics on Collective Motion*. His research focuses on 
learning theory for interacting particle systems and data-driven methods for 
dynamical systems.
