Collaborators and advisors I work with

Assistant Professor

387 Science Center <\p> <\p>Department of Mathematics

Clarkson University

Dr. James Greene is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Clarkson University and my PhD advisor. His research lies at the intersection of mathematical biology, dynamical systems, and optimal control theory, with a particular focus on modeling cancer treatment strategies and understanding collective behavior in biological systems.

My dissertation, Dynamics on Collective Motion, is developed under his guidance, exploring data-driven approaches to learning interaction kernels and environmental forces in agent-based systems.

Assistant Professor

Dr. Ming Zhong is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Johns Hopkins University and a research collaborator on my dissertation, Dynamics on Collective Motion. His research focuses on learning theory for interacting particle systems and data-driven methods for dynamical systems.

Associate Professor

Department of Mathematics

Clarkson University

Dr. Marko Budišić is an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Clarkson University. His research focuses on dynamical systems, ergodic theory, and data-driven methods for understanding complex flows and transport phenomena.

As my previous advisor, Dr. Budišić guided my early research in persistent homology applied to two-dimensional vortex dynamics, which was presented as a poster at the SIAM Conference on Applications of Dynamical Systems (DS23) in Portland, 2023.
